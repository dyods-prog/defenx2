Privacy Policy

At Defenx, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is kept secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you use our Defenx – Security App. We want to be transparent about the information we collect and how we use it, so you can make informed decisions about your privacy while using our app. By using our app, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy. We encourage you to read this policy carefully to understand our views and practices regarding your personal data.

Information We Collect

  • -Personal Information: This includes your email address (for customer support), device details (such as device model, OS version, and device ID), IP address (for security features), app usage statistics, and crash reports.
  • Non-Personal Information: This includes general usage data, anonymous analytics, and security logs (such as detected threats or unauthorized access).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • -Provide and improve the functionality of the app
  • -Detect, prevent, and respond to security threats and unauthorized access
  • -Analyze app performance, identify issues, and enhance user experience
  • -Communicate with you regarding support or app updates

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. Only authorized personnel have access to your data, and we make every effort to keep your information secure.

Third-Party Services

The app may contain links or integrations with third-party services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those services, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app or on our website. Your continued use of the app after changes to this policy implies your acceptance of the updated terms. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us via the support section in the app.
